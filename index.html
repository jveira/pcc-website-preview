How it works

PCC does not arrive with solutions. It backs the Colombian leaders already doing the work.

Children ages 5–17 in under-resourced rural areas. Paola Arenas Duarte in Cabrera, Jorge Esteban Suárez and Andrea González Londoño in Pereira — they knew their communities long before PCC arrived. More about the organization.

Pereira, RisaraldaClose to 200 children Alpha FC Un Sueño Que Construye Sueños — fútbol sala reaching close to 200 children across five communities, subsidized in full by Powerful Children Colombia.
Cabrera, Santander35 children Caminos Nativos Pedalea con Conciencia — mountain biking in Cabrera, Santander. Participation requires staying enrolled in school. Currently serves 35 children.
