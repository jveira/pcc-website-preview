How it works

PCC does not arrive with solutions. It backs the Colombian leaders already doing the work.

Two programs, run by Colombian organizations in rural communities. PCC funds them and helps design them. It does not run them. Children in under-resourced rural areas. Paola Arenas Duarte in Cabrera, Jorge Esteban Suárez and Andrea González Londoño in Pereira — they knew their communities long before PCC arrived. More about the organization.
