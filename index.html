Paola Arenas Duarte · Caminos Nativos

Pedalea con Conciencia.

Paola Arenas Duarte founded Caminos Nativos in Santander in 2017. Pedalea con Conciencia is its mountain biking program in Cabrera. It currently serves 35 children.

A note from Colombia · August 10

A 7.4 magnitude earthquake struck western Colombia on August 10, with its epicenter in San José del Palmar, Chocó. We are thinking of the families and communities affected. Our partner programs continue, and donations support that ongoing work. Support the programs

How PCC helps

PCC backs the Colombian leaders already doing the work.

Supporting Colombian-led efforts that enable all children to embrace their individual power and potential. Powerful Children Colombia partners with Colombian-led organizations to help design and fund programs for children.
