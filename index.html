Our Partner · Caminos Nativos

Pedalea con Conciencia.

Paola Arenas Duarte founded Caminos Nativos in Santander in 2017. PCC helped co-design and fully fund Pedalea con Conciencia, its mountain biking program in Cabrera. It currently serves 35 children.

PCC backs the Colombian leaders already doing the work. They knew their communities long before PCC arrived.

The programs are already running.Support decides how far they reach.

A note from Colombia · August 10

A 7.4 magnitude earthquake struck western Colombia on August 10, with its epicenter in San José del Palmar, Chocó. Our hearts are with the families and communities affected. PCC is raising funds to help our partners and their communities recover from the earthquake, and to support locally led emergency relief in affected areas.

How PCC helps

PCC partners with Colombian-led organizations already doing the work on the ground. We help design and fund their programs. Then we support them as they grow. We don't run programs ourselves. We back the leaders who do.
