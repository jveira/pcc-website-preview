About PCC

Supporting Colombian-led efforts that enable all children to embrace their individual power and potential.

PCC works with children living in under-resourced areas in Colombia. Research shows that supporting children while they are growing physically, mentally, and socially can make all the difference in building their confidence and self-esteem.

Why PCC exists

Focus & origins

By investing in under-resourced communities that have otherwise been largely ignored, PCC hopes to strengthen the capacity of trusted local leaders who are already making a meaningful impact on the ground, committed to supporting children in Colombia through athletic, cultural, and other educational opportunities that lead to economic and social stability.

Colombia has one of the highest levels of income inequality in the world and the second highest among 18 countries in Latin America and the Caribbean.

Recent studies and reporting have shown that youth in rural parts of Colombia who have less access to quality education and social services are more vulnerable to joining armed forces, turning to prostitution, or going into the workforce at an early age to support their families.

How PCC works

Values

01

Trust

+

We prioritize integrity, responsibility, rigor, and transparency when working with donors, Colombian-led orgs and leaders, and our broader community of supporters.

02

Inclusion

+

We value diversity and ground our work in humility, compassion, and solidarity. Our aim is to intentionally add an inclusion lens to everything we do.

03

Joy

+

We bring a joyful and child-like spirit to the work we do. This drives our resiliency and keeps us grounded in our commitment to supporting positive opportunities for youth to thrive.

04

Collaboration

+

We cannot do this work without the partnership of donors, Colombian-led organizations, the global Colombian community, volunteers, and formal and informal advisors. Community-building is core to our work.

05

Authenticity

+

We are unapologetically ourselves. This includes being courageous and creative in our approach, grounded in our values, and staying connected to our mission of enabling youth to embrace their power.

The team

Kathy Suarez

President

Biography+

Kathy was born in Bogotá, Colombia, and moved to the U.S. at age 12. She studied Finance, Communication, and Retail Studies at Santa Clara University. She worked in management consulting for a decade, helping organizations across the U.S. and U.K. through systemic cultural and organizational change. In 2016, as a self-taught DJ, she started an LA chapter of a grassroots organization focused on bringing more women, people of color, and LGBTQIA+ people into the music industry. That experience led her into a career in Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion, where she's led efforts at The Walt Disney Company and Netflix. She is currently a Senior Program Manager at Netflix, supporting the LATAM Talent leadership team in designing and implementing the region's Talent strategy.

Hannah Mora

Secretary

Biography+

Hannah was born in the U.S. to her American mother and Colombian father, who immigrated to the U.S. as a young adult. She served in the Jesuit Volunteer Corps and worked at nonprofits in Los Angeles and New York City serving individuals experiencing homelessness, survivors of domestic violence, sex trafficking, and related forms of gender violence, and individuals and families with low-income.
