Earthquake relief

Support earthquake relief

A 7.4 magnitude earthquake struck western Colombia on August 10, with its epicenter in San José del Palmar, Chocó. Chocó, Pereira, Manizales, and Cali have all been hit hard, and the situation is still unfolding. Our hearts are with the families, communities, and partners we work alongside there.

We're raising funds to:

  • Support our partners as they navigate the earthquake's impact on their staff, programs, and the communities they serve.
  • Fund locally led disaster response in Chocó, Pereira, Manizales, and Cali, guided by leaders on the ground we know and trust.

We'll share updates on how these funds are being used as our response takes shape. #PowerfulJuntos
