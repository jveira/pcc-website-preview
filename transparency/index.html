How PCC reports

PCC publishes figures alongside the report they come from. Alpha FC's program is subsidized in full by PCC. Pedalea con Conciencia has been fully funded by Julio Jaramillo and Evergreen Communities, LLC for two years running. Más allá del tablero's pilot phase is subsidized in full by PCC. Powerful Children Colombia is a certified 501(c)(3) organization.
