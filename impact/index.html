2025 Year in Review

Of course, numbers tell only part of the story.

Now in our fourth year, the growth is real: we've gone from reaching 21 children in our first year to 233 in 2025. Already in 2026, we've added a new partner organization, Más Que Vencedores, and are reaching 335 children. But what keeps us energized is what's compounding underneath those numbers. Two of Pedalea con Conciencia's original riders are now competing at a higher level and mentoring the kids coming up behind them. This year, for the second time, families from five communities came together at Alpha FC's family event. And our partners keep evolving with the children they serve. This includes new mental health workshops for Caminos Nativos parents and a dedicated initiative for Alpha FC's 15 to 17 year olds. Four years in, this is starting to feel less like a program and more like an ecosystem. That's exactly why we're so energized for 2026.
