What changed

Numbers tell only part of the story.

Now in its fourth year, PCC has watched children in these programs grow alongside the organization. Younger siblings are becoming eligible and joining, creating multi-generational impact. Graduating participants are staying on as volunteers.

Families are forming networks of support and care that extend beyond program activities. The community leaders PCC partners with continue to innovate how they support children and connect with their communities.
