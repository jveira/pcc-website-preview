2025 Year in Review

Of course, numbers tell only part of the story.

Of course, numbers tell only part of the story. Now in our fourth year of operations, we've watched the children participating in these programs grow alongside Powerful Children Colombia. Younger siblings are becoming eligible and joining the programs, creating multi-generational impact. Graduating participants are staying on as volunteers. Families are forming networks of support and care that extend beyond program activities.
